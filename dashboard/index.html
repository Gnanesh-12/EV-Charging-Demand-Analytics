Morning Peak Dominance
The 06–10 window carries 43% of all sessions and 45% of energy, at 4.4 arrivals/hour on active weekdays. This creates a simultaneous-arrival surge that sets daily occupancy for the rest of the day.
Weekday vs Weekend Gap
Weekdays average 48.3 sessions/day (peak Tue 50.8) versus 20.5 on weekends — a 2.4× difference driven by commuter patterns. Dispersion index: 10.2 weekdays, 13.5 weekends (1.0 = random arrivals).
Clustered, Not Random
Dispersion index 10.2 on weekdays (1.0 = Poisson random). Arrivals arrive in waves, not uniformly. Infrastructure must plan for burst capacity, not just average load.
COVID Recovery
Volume fell to 2% of the paid pre-COVID level in May 2020. A 106-day data gap (Aug–Nov 2020) coincides with campus closure. By Aug 2021 demand recovered to 65% of the pre-COVID paid level.

Capacity Planning
Buffer for 90th Percentile Days
Add +6 sessions/day to the point forecast (90th percentile of past under-forecasts) to cover 90% of days. At peak, about 26 chargers are needed on weekdays, 15 on weekends — within the 36 in recent use.
Energy Outlook
≈300 kWh / Day Expected
Weekly volume grows +7% from the first to the last forecast week as the drift term continues the recovery trend. If pre-COVID paid demand returns, busy weekdays need ≈37 chargers (within the 55 installed).
Forecast Validity
Use for 1–4 Weeks, Refit Weekly
Error in week 4 is 1.5× week 1. Refit the model each week as new data arrive. Weeks 5–8 are indicative only — not validated by the backtest.
Regime Change Protocol
Pricing Change Breaks the Model
Any pricing or access change starts a new regime. Switch immediately to the 8-week same-weekday mean as a fallback and retrain the model within 7 days of new-regime data.

Commuters Block Chargers
Workday commuters (06–10 arrivals, ≥6 h stay) are 28% of sessions but hold 45% of charger-hours and 50% of idle time. Idle fees or move-car alerts free capacity without new hardware.
Quick Top-ups Are Most Efficient
Short stays (<2 h) deliver 5.94 kWh per charger-hour vs 1.13 for commuters — a 5.3× efficiency advantage. Protecting short-stay bays (especially evenings and weekends) serves more drivers per slot.
Morning Arrivals Stay All Day
70% of 6–10 h sessions arrive in the 06–10 window (lift 1.69×, holding in 4/4 regimes). These block chargers until afternoon — peak occupancy (10–16 h) is driven by parked cars, not active charging demand.
Policy Shapes Behaviour
130 of 295 rules are policy-driven (max lift 6.09×). The 30-minute stop for unclaimed sessions in R3 appears directly in energy patterns. Re-mine rules after any pricing change.
