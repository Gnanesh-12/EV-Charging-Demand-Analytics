Morning Peak Dominance
06–10 window accounts for 43% of all sessions and 45% of energy. Average 4.4 arrivals per hour during this block on active weekdays.
📈
Weekday vs Weekend Gap
Weekdays average 48.3 sessions/day (Tue peak: 50.8) vs only 20.5 on weekends – a 2.4× difference driven by commuter patterns.

Dispersion Index
Demand is clustered, not random: weekday dispersion index 10.2, weekend 13.5 (1 = pure random arrivals). Plan for burst capacity.

COVID Recovery
By Aug 2021 demand was at 65% of pre-COVID paid level. A 106-day data gap (Aug–Nov 2020) coincides with campus closure.

Top Headline Association Rules

Rule Theme Support Confidence Lift Sessions/week Regimes
🚗
Commuters Block Chargers
Workday commuters (06–10 arrivals, ≥6h stay) are 28% of sessions but hold 45% of charger-hours and 50% of idle time. Idle fees or move-car alerts free capacity without new hardware.
⚡
Quick Top-ups Most Efficient
Short stays (<2h) deliver 5.94 kWh per charger-hour vs only 1.13 for commuters. Protecting a few short-stay bays (esp. evenings and weekends) serves more drivers per slot.
🌅
Morning Arrivals Stay All Day
70% of 6–10h sessions arrive in the 06–10 window. These sessions block chargers until afternoon – peak occupancy (10:00–16:00) is driven by parked cars, not power demand.
📏
Policy Shapes Behaviour
130 of 295 rules are policy-driven (max lift 6.09×). The 30-minute stop for unclaimed sessions in R3 shows up directly in energy patterns. Re-mine rules after any pricing change.
